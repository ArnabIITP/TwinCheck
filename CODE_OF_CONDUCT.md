# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The TwinCheck community aims to be welcoming, respectful and constructive. We want participation to be safe and productive for people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code and behavior rather than people.
- Acknowledge mistakes, learn from them and help repair their impact.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep discussions relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, demeaning comments or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Threats, stalking or sustained unwanted contact.
- Other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or hostile.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and project-related events or communications. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Do not include private information in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may remove or edit comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude someone from project spaces when necessary.

Maintainers will consider the nature and impact of the behavior, relevant context and any prior pattern. People asked to stop unacceptable behavior are expected to comply immediately.
